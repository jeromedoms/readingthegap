insights Overview

The literacy gap is wide, unequal, and still deeply persistent.

The study brings together three connected views: where reading difficulty is most visible, what systemic conditions sustain it, and which interventions offer the strongest measured gains.

SOP 1

Extent of reading difficulty

Examines non-readiness by grade level, proficiency by school classification, and overall proficiency distribution.

SOP 2

Systemic factors

Identifies structural pressures such as learning poverty, teacher mismatch, instructional loss, and developmental risk.

SOP 3

Intervention strategies

Compares reading interventions through pretest, posttest, and mean gain outcomes.

fact_check Key findings

Quick reads from the study

These cards summarize the major patterns before you move into the full SOP dashboards.

Finding 1

Reading difficulty remains high across later grade levels.

The problem is not confined to early elementary years. It continues into higher grade levels, suggesting weak recovery from early reading gaps.

Finding 2

School context shapes proficiency outcomes.

Proficiency varies sharply across school classifications, with already disadvantaged contexts showing the weakest outcomes.

Finding 3

Interventions show that improvement is still possible.

Several approaches produced positive gains, especially those combining structure, creativity, collaboration, and multimodal support.

task_alt Recommendations

Direction for policy and practice

The findings suggest that literacy improvement requires both targeted instructional action and broader system support.

location_on Prioritize marginalized school contexts

Direct stronger literacy support to Last Mile, GIDA, Hardship Post, and IP education settings.

school Reduce teacher mismatch

Improve subject alignment and literacy-focused teacher development to strengthen classroom instruction.

event_repeat Protect instructional time

Reduce the long-term effect of recurring instructional disruption through continuity planning.

rocket_launch Scale effective interventions

Expand strategies that show strong gains, especially multimodal and scaffolded approaches.

restaurant Support early nutrition and readiness

Address developmental barriers that affect cognition, language, and early reading acquisition.

music_note Use accessible motivational approaches

Integrate lower-cost strategies such as music and motivation-oriented reading support where resources are limited.
